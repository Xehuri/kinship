## What counts as commercial use

Commercial use includes, but is not limited to:

- selling the kernel or a product or service whose value depends on it;
- embedding the kernel in a paid product or hosted service;
- using the kernel to deliver paid consulting, integration, or support;
- internal use by a for-profit organization in its revenue-generating
  operations.

## How to request a commercial license

Contact the project maintainers through the repository issue tracker and
describe the intended use, deployment scale, and distribution model. A
separate written agreement is required before any commercial use begins.
